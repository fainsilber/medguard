## Full description

```
MedGuard helps families keep complicated medication routines safe, on time, and in sync between everyone who helps care for a loved one.

Built for households where several people give medicine — parents, grandparents, partners, relatives — and where a missed or doubled dose really matters.

SHARED BY THE WHOLE HOUSEHOLD
• Everyone in the household sees the same schedule and history, updated in real time.
• Tap "Taken" on one phone and it is instantly marked on everyone else's, so nobody gives the same dose twice.
• Every dose records who gave it and when.
• Join a household with a simple code — no passwords to remember at 3 AM.

ALARMS YOU CAN'T SLEEP THROUGH
• A dose alarm that rings at alarm volume, even on a locked phone with the screen off.
• Mark a dose Taken or Snooze it straight from the notification.
• The alarm stops on its own after a set time, and you choose how long it rings.
• If a dose isn't confirmed, the app can alert the other caregivers so it isn't missed.

FLEXIBLE SCHEDULES
• Daily, every few days, specific days of the week, or alternating doses (for example 50 mg on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 25 mg the other days).
• Short courses with a start and end date, such as a five-day taper.
• Change a dose whenever the protocol changes — future doses update, and past history stays untouched.

SAFETY GUARDS FOR AS-NEEDED MEDICINE
• Set a minimum time between doses and a daily maximum for as-needed medicines.
• A clear green or red status shows at a glance whether the next dose is safe to give.
• When a medicine is locked, the app shows how long is left and asks you to confirm twice before overriding.
• See who gave the last dose, and when.

NEVER RUN OUT
• Stock is counted down automatically each time a dose is logged.
• Set a low-stock level and every caregiver is alerted when it's time to reorder.
• Record refills and adjustments in seconds.

SHABBAT AND YOM TOV MODE
• Made for observant households: on Shabbat and Yom Tov the app needs no taps and no screen.
• Dose alerts play a short chime that stops by itself.
• After Havdalah, a single reconciliation screen lets you confirm the doses that were given and add any as-needed doses in one go.

WORKS WHEN THE SIGNAL DOESN'T
• Your medicines, schedules and history are stored on your phone, so the app works offline.
• Anything you log offline is saved and syncs automatically once you're back online.

YOUR RECORDS, YOUR CONTROL
• Logs are append-only. A correction is added as a new entry instead of overwriting the old one, so the history can be trusted.
• Export your records at any time, or back them up and restore them.
• Your dosing history is not copied to Google Drive by Android's automatic backup.

Important: MedGuard is a scheduling, reminder and record-keeping tool. It does not give medical advice, and it doesn't replace your doctor, pharmacist or care team. Always follow their instructions for your medicines and doses.
```
